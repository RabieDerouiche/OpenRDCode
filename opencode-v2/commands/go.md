## 1. Ticket and directives
- The key: the first word of the request when it looks like `ABC-123`; otherwise the key of the current branch ("Jira key from branch" line); otherwise ask, in ONE question tool call (header "Ticket"), « Quel ticket ? (ou écris ce qu'il faut faire, sans ticket Jira) », among my open tickets of the active sprint (Jira, read-only: `assignee = currentUser() AND sprint in openSprints() AND statusCategory != Done ORDER BY Rank ASC`, project of AGENTS.md "Daily" when it is set; 5 results, the fields `summary,status` only): up to 3 options `<KEY> - <summary>`, in-progress first, the first one « (recommandé) », then « Sans ticket » (« un changement sans ticket Jira : tu le décris, /go le fait jusqu'à la MR »). That search fails or finds nothing (never another JQL): the same question, « Aucun ticket ouvert pour toi dans le sprint : écris une clé (ABC-123), ou ce qu'il faut faire sans ticket », with « Sans ticket » and « Rien pour l'instant » as options. My answer: a key → that ticket; a text that is neither an option nor a key → the request becomes « sans ticket : <that text> » (below); « Sans ticket » → ONE more question (header « Changement »): « Que faut-il faire, sans ticket ? (quoi, où, pourquoi) », with the option « Rien pour l'instant »: my text becomes « sans ticket : <my text> »; « Rien pour l'instant », or no answer → stop with « ⛔ Donne la clé du ticket (/go ABC-123) ou ce qu'il faut faire (/go sans ticket : …) ». With « sans question » in the directives and no key: that stop, without a question.
- « sans ticket » at the start of the request (`/go sans ticket : <what to do>`, or `/go sans ticket sur <branch> : <what to do>`): a change without a Jira ticket. The text after it describes the change (what, where, why) and replaces the ticket, verbatim, for the analyst. No Jira call at all. Its key, `LOCAL-<n>`, comes from branch.sh in step 3 and names its ticket sheet only, never the branch: the branch keeps a name of its own (the one a « sur <branch> » directive names, the branch of its own I am on, or a new branch named after the change).
- Findings: when this session investigated the problem before this /go (my messages and the answers above it), their conclusions (cause, files, evidence, a reproducing test) go to the analyst.
- Directives: the rest of the request. They reach every subagent prompt. Recognize:
  - « comme le pattern <name> »: a pattern skill of the team, given to the analyst.
  - « comme dans <folder> » (a path, or the alias of a reference of opencode.json): a reference implementation in another repository. A path that is not a configured reference: your first tool call, ALONE in its turn, is one `glob` with pattern `*` on that folder, after the line « Lecture de <folder> : réponds « Toujours » à la demande d'accès (une fois pour toute la session OpenCode). » Refused or not found: go on without it and say so in the final answer.
  - « analyse seule »: stop after step 4 (the developer reviews the plan; the next /go codes it).
  - « pas à pas »: stop after each plan step.
  - « sans question » (also « vite », « en autonomie »): I am away for this run, nothing waits for my answer: every question of this procedure takes its recommended option instead of being asked (the blocking questions of the analyst: their recommended answer, recorded `ticket-sheet.sh decision "<topic> : <answer> (choix recommandé, sans question : à valider en review)"`; a business question for the product owner: the same, never « Je demande au PO », and « Points d'attention » names it; « Tests unitaires », « Composants » and « Cible »: their recommended option, recorded as their « Oui », « Non » or « Mapperly » says). The merge request lists these choices first in « Décisions prises (à valider en review) ». Only the « Suite » question at the end is asked.
  - « refais l'analyse »: step 4 again on an existing sheet (its decisions and its log are kept).
  - « sur <branch> », « dans <branch> », « sur la branche <branch> » (a branch of mine, here or on origin): the work goes on that branch, as it is named, with a ticket or without: in step 3, `--on <branch>` for branch.sh, which records the key for it (the sheet is found from it; the branch is never renamed). Not « depuis <branch> », below, which starts a new branch from it.
  - « depuis <branch> », « à partir de <branch> », « part de <branch> », « sur la base de <branch> » (a teammate's branch, a release branch): the base of the ticket branch instead of develop. In step 3, `--from <branch>` for branch.sh: a new ticket branch starts from origin/<branch>, an existing one keeps its commits; either way the branch is recorded as its base (the "BASE:" line, then the "Base branch" line of the workflow state): the measure of step 4, the review, the check before the push, the merges of `ship.sh --base` and the target of the merge request. Never look at the branches yourself, never merge it by hand: a "STOP:" line of branch.sh (no such branch on origin, with the close names) stops the run with it.
  - « merge <branch> », « intègre <branch> », « fusionne avec <branch> » (a hotfix, a teammate's branch): in step 2, `sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/ship.sh --sync-only --base --merge <branch>`. The script fetches, merges, and lists the conflicting files: never look at the branches, their merge base or their files yourself. A "STOP:" line: stop with it (a conflict is resolved by the developer in Rider). Then the run goes on as usual, and a delivered ticket goes to steps 6 and 7 (full verification, then delivery: the push updates the merge request).
