Take the ticket from its analysis to its Draft merge request, in this single run. The developer is not watching: follow your autonomy contract.

## 1. Ticket and directives
- The key: the first word of the request when it looks like `ABC-123`; otherwise the key of the current branch ("Jira key from branch" line); otherwise ask, in ONE question tool call (header "Ticket"), among my open tickets of the active sprint (Jira, read-only: `assignee = currentUser() AND sprint in openSprints() AND statusCategory != Done ORDER BY Rank ASC`, project of AGENTS.md "Daily" when it is set): up to 4 options `<KEY> - <summary>`, in-progress first, the first one « (recommandé) ». That search fails or finds nothing: stop with « ⛔ Donne la clé du ticket : /go ABC-123 », never another JQL.
- « sans ticket » at the start of the request (`/go sans ticket : <what to do>`): a change without a Jira ticket. The text after it describes the change (what, where, why) and replaces the ticket, verbatim, for the analyst. No Jira call at all. Its key comes from branch.sh in step 3: `LOCAL-<n>`.
- Findings: when this session investigated the problem before this /go (my messages and the answers above it), their conclusions (cause, files, evidence, a reproducing test) go to the analyst.
- Directives: the rest of the request. They reach every subagent prompt. Recognize:
  - « comme le pattern <name> »: a pattern skill of the team, given to the analyst.
  - « comme dans <folder> » (a path, or the alias of a reference of opencode.json): a reference implementation in another repository. A path that is not a configured reference: your first tool call, ALONE in its turn, is one `glob` with pattern `*` on that folder, after the line « Lecture de <folder> : réponds « Toujours » à la demande d'accès (une fois pour toute la session OpenCode). » Refused or not found: go on without it and say so in the final answer.
  - « analyse seule »: stop after step 4 (the developer reviews the plan; the next /go codes it).
  - « pas à pas »: stop after each plan step.
  - « refais l'analyse »: step 4 again on an existing sheet (its decisions and its log are kept).
  - « depuis <branch> », « à partir de <branch> », « part de <branch> », « sur la base de <branch> » (a teammate's branch, a release branch): the base of the ticket branch instead of develop. In step 3, `--from <branch>` for branch.sh: a new ticket branch starts from origin/<branch>, an existing one keeps its commits; either way the branch is recorded as its base (the "BASE:" line, then the "Base branch" line of the workflow state): the measure of step 4, the review, the check before the push, the merges of `ship.sh --base` and the target of the merge request. Never look at the branches yourself, never merge it by hand: a "STOP:" line of branch.sh (no such branch on origin, with the close names) stops the run with it.
  - « merge <branch> », « intègre <branch> », « fusionne avec <branch> » (a hotfix, a teammate's branch): in step 2, `sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/ship.sh --sync-only --base --merge <branch>`. The script fetches, merges, and lists the conflicting files: never look at the branches, their merge base or their files yourself. A "STOP:" line: stop with it (a conflict is resolved by the developer in Rider). Then the run goes on as usual, and a delivered ticket goes to steps 6 and 7 (full verification, then delivery: the push updates the merge request).

## 2. Before any work
- "Fetch:" says AUTH FAILED: stop (your stop conditions): nothing could be pushed at the end.
- "Merge in progress: yes": stop with « ⛔ Une fusion est en cours sur la branche : termine-la dans Rider (ou git merge --abort), puis relance /go. »
- A « merge <branch> » directive: the merge now (see step 1), recorded with `ticket-sheet.sh suivi "/go - merge de <branch>"`.

## 3. Resume or start
- On the branch of the key with its ticket sheet printed above: resume, and record it first: `sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/ticket-sheet.sh suivi "/go - reprise à l'étape <N>"` (or « reprise : vérification », « reprise : livraison »). Unchecked plan steps → step 5 from the first one. Every step checked and no « /go - livré » line in « ## Suivi » → step 6. Already delivered, with a directive that asks for work (a merge, a change): that work, then steps 6 and 7. Already delivered otherwise → answer « ✅ Déjà livré (MR <link from the sheet>) : /fix pour la CI ou la review » and stop. « refais l'analyse » → step 4.
- Files listed under "Uncommitted files" when you resume: an interrupted attempt left them. They go to the prompt of the next coder (step 5.2).
- Otherwise, start: read the ticket summary and type with the Jira tools (one call); without a ticket, no Jira call: the summary is a title of 6 words at most that you write from the description, the type bugfix for a defect, otherwise feature, and the key `LOCAL`. Then in ONE shell call: `sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/branch.sh <KEY> <feature | bugfix for a Bug | hotfix for an urgent production incident> "<ticket summary>" [--from <branch>] && sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/workflow-state.sh --no-fetch --sheet` (`--from` only with a « depuis <branch> » directive). A "STOP:" line: stop with it. Without a ticket, its "KEY:" line gives the key from then on. A "BASE:" line: once the sheet exists, record it with `ticket-sheet.sh decision "Base : <branch> au lieu de develop (consigne du dev)"`. The sheet of the key exists: resume as above; otherwise step 4.
- Resuming with a « depuis <branch> » directive: `sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/branch.sh <KEY> feature "<ticket summary>" --from <branch>` once, before the rest (it records the base of the existing branch).

## 4. Analysis, with a measure of develop in parallel
One line « Analyse du ticket, et build + tests de develop en parallèle… », then in ONE turn, both together:
- the `analyst` subagent with the subagent tool: "Analyze Jira ticket <KEY> and write its ticket sheet (without a ticket: « Analyze the change <KEY>, which has no Jira ticket, and write its ticket sheet. Description of the change, by the developer: <the description, verbatim>. »). Ticket sheet to write: <the path of the "Current ticket sheet" line>. Repository root: <Repository root line>. Branch: <branch>. Project type: <type from AGENTS.md "Project type" or the detection above>. Now: <Now line>. Developer's directives: <directives or « aucune »>. Findings of this session before /go: <cause, files, evidence, or « aucun »>. Pattern named by the developer: <name or « aucun »>. Reference repository named by the developer: <absolute folder, or « aucun »>. Follow your procedure and answer in your format."
- the full suite of the base, the measure of develop: `sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/build-test.sh` with the solution or project and the options of AGENTS.md "Build & test" (front-end: its test command). Not when the project is legacy .NET Framework (it does not build here), not on « refais l'analyse » when the branch already has commits ahead of the base.

Then:
1. The measure: "ENVIRONMENT PROBLEM": stop (your stop conditions). "BUILD: FAILED": when the ticket is about these errors (its summary, its description or the plan of the sheet names the build, a compilation error or the files of these errors), record `ticket-sheet.sh suivi "/go - base develop : build en échec (<first error line>) - c'est l'objet du ticket"` and go on; otherwise stop with « ⛔ <the base> ne compile pas sur cette machine : <first error line>. Ce ticket ne porte pas sur cette erreur : corrige d'abord la base (un ticket, ou /go sans ticket : …), puis relance /go <KEY>. » Otherwise record it: `sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/ticket-sheet.sh suivi "/go - base develop : build ok - tests <passed>/<total> - en échec avant le ticket : <the names of the FAILED TESTS line, or aucun>"`. These tests already fail without the ticket: never yours to fix, listed in the merge request.
2. The sheet, in ONE turn: `sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/ticket-sheet.sh check` and the read of the sheet (its path is the FICHE line of the analyst). No sheet: retry the analyst once; still none: stop. "problem(s)": one more `analyst` round ("Fix these problems of the ticket sheet <path>, and only them: <the problem lines>."), then check again; still problems: go on if the plan has numbered steps, otherwise stop.
3. QUESTIONS BLOQUANTES > 0: ask them ALL in ONE question tool call (header "Ticket ambigu" or a short topic), one question per blocking question, its options as given, the recommended one first, plus a last option « Je demande au PO » for business questions. Record each answer: `sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/ticket-sheet.sh decision "<topic> : <answer> (réponse du dev)"`, all chained with `&&` in one call. « Je demande au PO » on a question: record `/go - en attente du PO : <question>` with `ticket-sheet.sh suivi`, and stop: the line after the header is « ⏸ En attente du PO : <question> », then that /go resumes once I have the answer (I give it in the directives: `/go <KEY> <the answer>`).
   A plan step « (test d'abord) » in a .NET project whose measure of develop says « TESTS: NONE RUN » (no unit test project): one question (header « Tests unitaires »; in the same call as the blocking questions, when there are some): « Ce dépôt n'a pas de projet de tests unitaires : le bug ne peut pas être prouvé par un test. Je le crée en premier commit ? » with « Oui, xUnit (recommandé) », « Oui, MSTest », « Oui, NUnit », « Non, sans projet de tests ». « Oui », before step 5, in ONE shell call: `sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/test-guardian.sh setup <xunit | mstest | nunit> <the files of the « (test d'abord) » steps> && sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/ship.sh --no-push "test: add the unit test project (<KEY>)" "- <the SETUP line, from the project to the packages>"`, and `ticket-sheet.sh decision "Tests unitaires : projet <its name> créé (réponse du dev)"`. A "STOP:" line: go on without it (setup undid everything) and say it in « Points d'attention »; an "ENVIRONMENT PROBLEM" line: stop.
4. « analyse seule »: answer « ✅ Analyse prête : <N> étapes », the plan as a numbered list, the decisions, and « Relis la fiche `<path of the sheet>` (corrige-la si besoin), puis /go <KEY> pour coder. » Stop.

## 5. Plan steps, one subagent each
For each unchecked step of « ## Plan », in order:
1. A step that ends with « (test d'abord) » fixes a bug: in a .NET project that builds here and has a unit test project, its test is written first by the `test-guardian`, who never fixes it (the test does not follow the fix). One line « Étape <N>/<total> : test du bug… », then the `test-guardian` (description « Étape <N>/<total> : test du bug »): "Mission « bug », phase « avant le correctif ». Repository root: <root>. Test project: <the unit test project of these files>. Coverage label: S<N>. Tests that already fail on develop (never yours): <names or « aucun »>. Files already modified before you started (an interrupted attempt: tests of yours to check again with red, keep or fix them): <list, or « aucun »>. Ticket sheet (read-only): <path>." Its report OK with « RED »: keep its « Filtre » line and its sessionID for 2 and 3. « non démontrable » or BLOQUÉ: the coder writes the test itself (its « (test d'abord) » rule), and the step goes on as any other. Not RED because its tests pass on the current code: the same session once, « Tes tests passent sur le code actuel : ils ne reproduisent pas le bug. 2. One line « Étape <N>/<total> : <step in a few words>… », then invoke the `coder` subagent with the subagent tool:
   "Implement step <N> of ticket <KEY> in the repository <Repository root>. Step: <full text of the step line>. Acceptance criteria concerned: <the C lines it serves>. Tests that already fail on develop (not yours, never touch them): <names from the base line, or « aucun »>. Developer's directives: <directives or « aucune »>. Project type: <type>. Build and tests: <AGENTS.md "Build & test", otherwise the detection above>. <After a test of the test-guardian: « Tests the test-guardian wrote for this bug, failing now: <its Filtre> in <test project>. 3. After a test of the test-guardian, the coder's report OK: the SAME test-guardian session (description « Étape <N>/<total> : contrôle du test »), « Phase « après le correctif ». » Its report OK (GREEN twice, no coverage lost): 4. ÉCHEC (a test still red, a flaky test, a lost coverage): one round of the SAME coder session with its lines, then this check again; still failing: as 5. A test the coder changed is put back by the test-guardian before its check (its seal), and the check is made on it.
4. RÉSULTAT OK (and, after a test of the test-guardian, its check OK): in ONE shell call (after a test of the test-guardian, it starts with `sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/test-guardian.sh sealed S<N> && `: a STOP means the test changed since it was sealed, nothing is committed, and 3 runs once more before this call), `sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/ship.sh --no-push "<type>(<scope>): <the step in English, imperative> (<KEY>)" "<one line per file group>"` then `&& sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/ticket-sheet.sh tick <N> "/go étape <N> - <files count> fichiers - <Build and Tests of the report, short>"`, followed by `&& sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/ticket-sheet.sh decision "<topic> : <choice> — <reason> (étape <N>)"` for each decision of the report. 5. RÉSULTAT ÉCHEC, a report without its RÉSULTAT line (the coder reached its step limit), or a subagent error (the subagent tool fails): invoke the `debugger` subagent: "Find the root cause of this failure in the repository <root>, for step <N> of ticket <KEY>: <the Problème line and the failing BUILD/TESTS lines of the coder, or the error>. Files of the step: <list>. Nobody answers questions during your work: if the cause is not certain, give your best hypothesis with its evidence." Then the SAME coder session again: the subagent tool with the `sessionID` of its report (given with its result) and the prompt « Diagnostic du debugger : <its root cause and proposed fix>. A BLOQUÉ whose reason is a wrong premise (an API, a type, a member, an option or a behavior that the step or a decision of the sheet takes for granted and that does not exist, or works otherwise: the coder's evidence is a compiler error or the package documentation): never investigate it yourself; one `debugger` round, description « Diagnostic étape <N> » ("The step <N> of ticket <KEY> rests on a wrong premise: <the Problème line of the coder>. Find what really exists and how this repository or the package does it: the XML documentation of the package (`sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/package-refs.sh --doc <package id>` gives its file; grep tool on the member names), its existing usages in the repository, the AGENTS.md conventions. The debugger finds nothing usable, or the coder is still BLOQUÉ: stop as below.
   Still ÉCHEC, or BLOQUÉ at any time: record `ticket-sheet.sh suivi "/go - bloqué à l'étape <N> : <reason>"`, commit what builds with `ship.sh --no-push` only if the last report says the build is OK, and stop with your report.
6. « pas à pas »: stop after this step: « ✅ Étape <N>/<total> faite (pas à pas) » and its report; the next /go continues.

## 6. Verification of the whole branch
1. One line « Build et tests complets, couverture des changements et auto-review… », then in ONE turn, both together (the machine runs the suite while the checker reads):
   - the full suite. .NET that builds here: `sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/test-guardian.sh changes <Base branch line> <solution or project of AGENTS.md "Build & test">`: the BUILD, TESTS and FAILED TESTS lines of build-test.sh, then the « CHANGED: » lines (the lines of the branch that no test runs) and the « COVERAGE OF THE CHANGES » line. Front-end or legacy: `sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/build-test.sh` or the commands of AGENTS.md, as before (no coverage);
   - the `checker` subagent (description « Auto-review »): "Self-review of the branch of ticket <KEY> in the repository <root>, before it is pushed. Diff: `git diff <Base branch line>...HEAD` (everything is committed). Ticket sheet: <path> (acceptance criteria, decisions). Project type: <type>. The full build and tests run in parallel with you: never run a build or tests, review by reading. Use your output for a self-review."
2. The suite: failed tests listed in the base line of « ## Suivi » are not the ticket's: they are not fixed, they go to the merge request. Any other failure: one `coder` round with the failing lines (prompt: "Fix these build or test failures of the branch of ticket <KEY>, without changing the expected behavior: <lines>. Tests that already fail on develop (not yours, never touch them): <names>…"), commit it with `ship.sh --no-push`, run the suite again; a new failure still there: stop as in step 5.5.
3. Coverage of the changes: « CHANGED: » lines with « not covered » ranges: one line « Tests des lignes non couvertes… », then the `test-guardian` (description « Couverture des changements »): "Mission « couverture ». Lines the branch of ticket <KEY> changed that no test runs: <the CHANGED lines>. Ticket sheet (read-only, the expected behavior): <path>. Repository root: <root>. Test projects: <the unit test projects of these files>. Coverage label: changes. Tests that already fail on develop (never yours): <names>." Its tests added: in ONE shell call, `ship.sh --no-push "test(<scope>): cover the changes of the ticket (<KEY>)"` then `build-test.sh` with the solution (the suite again, without coverage). A bug that a coverage test found (its « Problème » line): one `coder` round with it, the test-guardian's test as proof, then the same commit and suite. Record `ticket-sheet.sh suivi "/go - couverture des changements : <before> % -> <after> %"`. Nothing « not covered »: nothing to do.
4. The checker: it failed (a task error, or a report without its VERDICT line): resume it ONCE, the subagent tool with its `sessionID` and « Donne ton verdict maintenant, dans ton format, avec ce que tu as déjà lu. » Still nothing: go on without it, and « Vérifications » and « Points d'attention » of the merge request say « auto-review non faite : <reason> ». Never a third checker call. Points « bloquant » or « majeur », and criteria « non trouvé » or « non testé »: one `coder` round with all of them ("Fix these review findings of the branch of ticket <KEY>: <the numbered points and criteria>. Decisions already taken: <…>. Report in your format."), commit (`ship.sh --no-push "fix(<scope>): address the self-review findings (<KEY>)"`), then the full suite again (`build-test.sh`). At most 2 rounds in all; what remains goes to « Points d'attention » of the merge request. « mineur » and « question » points are not fixed: they go to the merge request.

## 7. Delivery
1. One line « Mise à jour avec develop… », then `sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/ship.sh --sync-only --base`: it brings, by merge, the commits pushed on the branch from another computer and the new commits of develop. A "STOP:" line: stop with it (a conflict is resolved by the developer in Rider, then /go delivers).
2. « REMOTE: » or « BASE: » says something was merged: the full suite again (`build-test.sh`), before anything is pushed.
3. One line « Contrôle et push… », then `sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/ship.sh "<type>(<scope>): <ticket summary in English> (<KEY>)"`: it commits what remains, checks the changes (GUARD lines), then pushes. "GUARD: STOP": stop with « ⛔ Contrôle avant push : rien n'a été poussé » and its lines, and « COMMANDES.md, section « Contrôle avant push » ». Another "STOP:" line: stop with it. Keep the WARNING and PACKAGES lines: they go to the merge request.
4. The merge request, as your rules say: find it, or create it as Draft with its description; otherwise give the MR LINK and the description to paste.
5. `sh ~/.config/opencode/scripts/ticket-sheet.sh suivi "/go - livré - MR <link> - build ok - tests <passed>/<total> - auto-review <verdict>"`.

## Answer, in French
- Header line, then « 🚀 MR en Draft : <link> » (or « 🚀 MR à créer : <link> »).
- « Étapes » table: | # | Étape | Fichiers | Tests | ✅ |.
- « Décisions prises (à valider en review) »: one line each, with its reason; the developer's answers first.

- « Points d'attention » when there are some (minor findings, questions of the review, packages added, warnings of the check, what is left).
